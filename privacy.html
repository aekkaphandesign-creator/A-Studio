Privacy Policy — A Studio

Effective date: 21 November 2026

A Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by Amply Agency ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses when a TikTok account is connected.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

- Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar. We use it only to show which account is connected.

- Permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload). We use it only to send videos we prepared to that account's TikTok inbox.

- Access tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can upload drafts for the connected account.

We do not request or receive the account's followers, messages, viewing history, analytics or any other data.

2. How the data is stored

All data is stored only on Amply Agency's own computer, in the app's local database. It is not stored on a public server, and tokens are never shown on screen or written to logs.

3. How the data is used

Only to identify the connected account and to upload drafts that we choose to send. We do not sell, rent or share the data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. When an account is disconnected, its tokens and profile information are deleted from the app. To ask for deletion, or to ask what we hold, email amplyagency.th@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke A Studio's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services, or the equivalent menu in your app version), or by disconnecting the account in A Studio.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and is not used to manage accounts of minors.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

8. Contact

Amply Agency — amplyagency.th@gmail.com
