A Studio

A Studio is a private content tool operated by Amply Agency. It helps us prepare short videos for our own channels and send finished videos to our own TikTok accounts as drafts. We review every draft and publish it ourselves in the TikTok app. A Studio never publishes on its own.

A Studio is used only by Amply Agency for accounts we own or manage. It is not offered to the public.
