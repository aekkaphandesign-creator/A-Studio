Terms of Service — A Studio

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. About A Studio

A Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by Amply Agency ("we", "us"). It prepares short videos and, when we choose, uploads a finished video to a connected TikTok account as a draft in that account's TikTok inbox.

2. Who can use it

The app is used only by Amply Agency to manage TikTok accounts that we own or are authorised to manage. It is not a public service and does not accept outside sign-ups.

3. What the app does on TikTok

- It signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) only after the account owner approves the connection.

- It uploads videos as drafts. It does not publish posts, comment, like, follow, or send messages.

- The account owner reviews each draft and publishes it, or deletes it, in the TikTok app.

4. Your responsibilities

Whoever connects a TikTok account must own or be authorised to manage it. All uploaded content must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including rules on branded content and paid partnership disclosure.

5. Disconnecting

A connected account can be disconnected at any time, either in A Studio or in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services, or the equivalent menu in your app version).

The app is provided "as is" for internal use. We are not liable for losses caused by service interruptions at TikTok or elsewhere.
